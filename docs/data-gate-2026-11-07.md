# Week 1 data gate — result (7 Nov 2026)

**Decision: build v1 on Haryana (HRERA Gurugram). Keep UP as phase 2.**

## Haryana — HRERA Gurugram

Source: public decisions table at `https://haryanarera.gov.in/admincontrol/judgements/2`
(no login, no CAPTCHA). Each row has complaint number, parties, type, decision date and a
link to the order PDF (`/assistancecontrol/viewOrderPdf/<id>`).

Snapshot of the table on 7 Nov 2026:

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Rows (all types) | 18,087 |
| Rows with an order PDF | 13,990 |
| "HRERA Complaints" with an order PDF | **13,270** |

Complaint orders with a PDF, by decision year:

| 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 (to date) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 451 | 1,086 | 562 | 1,182 | 1,509 | 1,845 | 2,092 | 3,871 | 1,392 |

That clears the plan's bar (3,000+ orders) several times over and gives ~3,900 orders
for a held-out 2025 test year with ~8,700 earlier orders to build on.
(Year counts include all complaint types; the "complaints with PDF" total is 13,270.)

### Sample test: 50 complaint orders, random across years, fetched 2.5 s apart

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Downloaded as valid PDF | 50 / 50 (0 errors) |
| Has a text layer (> 500 chars) | 27 / 50 |
| Scanned (no text) | 23 / 50 |
| Pages per order | 1 – 52, median 16 |
| Text orders mentioning delay possession / refund | 13 / 14 of 27 |
| Text orders citing the prescribed rate / MCLR | 17 of 27 |

**Important:** recent orders are mostly scans (2025: 11 of 15 sampled; 2026: 9 of 9).
OCR is therefore required, not optional, and the held-out 2025 year depends on it.
OCR accuracy must be measured on the 200-record hand check before the backtest is trusted.

## Uttar Pradesh — UP RERA

* Public "Important Judgement" list (`/JudgementView`, served by
  `WebService1.asmx/loadjudgement`): **8 orders only**.
* Per-complaint status and orders (`Frm_All_Complaint_Status_Page.aspx`) sit behind an
  image CAPTCHA. We will not automate around it.
* The two orders tested (10 and 16 pages) are scans with **no text layer**.
* `robots.txt` does not disallow these paths.

UP is not viable for v1 from public pages alone. Options for later: request bulk orders
from UP RERA, a licensed source, or cases users upload themselves.

## Fetching rules used (and to keep)

Slow, sequential requests (≥ 2.5 s apart), an identifying user agent, no login, no CAPTCHA
solving, every fetch logged. Downloaded files stay out of git.

## Reproduce
